Selected AI Engineering Mechanisms

Prompt system design

Translate business intent into reusable system, role, context, constraint, example, output-schema, and evaluation components; iterate against representative cases rather than one-off demonstrations.

Agentic process architecture

Separate responsibilities, tools, state, retrieval, human authority, exception handling, and escalation so agentic coding solutions remain modular, understandable, and aligned with SOLID principles.

Quality and security testing

Test expected outputs, ambiguity, edge cases, prompt-injection exposure, data boundaries, failure modes, abstention, traceability, and human-review triggers before scaling.

Automation value model

Baseline the current process and quantify cycle time, touch time, rework, exception rate, quality, decision latency, adoption, retained use, and organizational impact after change.

Hands-On AI Engineering Approach

1. Elicit & MapInterview process owners, clarify requirements, identify decision points, exceptions, current-state friction, and measurable baselines.
2. Prompt & PrototypeWrite, test, and iterate prompts; structure context; select agent, retrieval, automation, or assistive patterns; keep scope modular and SOLID.
3. Evaluate & SecureDefine expected outputs, quality checks, failure modes, security-testing considerations, human authority, escalation, and evidence needed for trust.
4. Embed & EducateIntegrate the smallest useful workflow, document standard work, demonstrate the solution, and communicate in technical and non-technical terms.
5. Measure ValueQuantify cycle time, touch time, rework, exception rate, quality, decision latency, adoption, retained use, and organizational impact.
6. Scale ResponsiblyConvert validated prompts and patterns into reusable assets, monitor performance, improve through feedback, and stop what does not create value.
