Selected AI engineering mechanisms

Prompt system design

Translate business intent into reusable role, context, constraint, example, output-schema, and evaluation components; iterate against representative cases.

Agentic process architecture

Separate responsibilities, tools, state, retrieval, human authority, exception handling, and escalation so workflows remain modular and understandable.

Quality and security testing

Test expected outputs, ambiguity, edge cases, prompt-injection exposure, data boundaries, failure modes, abstention, traceability, and review triggers.

Automation value model

Baseline cycle time, touch time, rework, exceptions, quality, decision latency, adoption, retained use, and organizational impact.

Hands-on AI engineering approach

1. Elicit & MapInterview process owners; clarify requirements, decisions, exceptions, friction, ownership, and measurable baselines.
2. Prompt & PrototypeWrite and iterate prompts; structure context; select assistive, retrieval, agent, or deterministic automation patterns.
3. Evaluate & SecureDefine expected outputs, edge cases, prompt-injection exposure, data boundaries, failure modes, and review triggers.
4. Embed & EducateIntegrate the smallest useful workflow, document standard work, demonstrate the solution, and train technical and business users.
5. Measure ValueQuantify cycle time, touch time, rework, exceptions, quality, decision latency, adoption, retained use, and completed outcomes.
6. Scale ResponsiblyConvert validated patterns into reusable assets; monitor, improve through feedback, and stop what does not create value.
