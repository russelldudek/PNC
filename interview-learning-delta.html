The AI Work Control Envelope — expanded for the factory

Clause 01

Business intent

Define the completed outcome, baseline friction, user, process owner, exceptions, and expected value.

Clause 02

Approved context

Identify systems of record, data classification, access inheritance, freshness, conflict, and no-answer behavior.

Clause 03

Tool authority

Separate retrieve, prepare, recommend, write, and execute; enforce least privilege, limits, and reversibility.

Clause 04

Evaluation evidence

Use versioned normal, edge, adversarial, permission, refusal, and recovery cases with business and risk partners.

Clause 05

Human decision

Keep consequential judgment, approval, exception handling, escalation, and stop authority with named people.

Clause 06

Operating record and economics

Observe sources, prompts, tools, interventions, completed outcomes, latency, cost, review burden, adoption, failure, and recovery.
