Stakeholder-informed revision

Discovery is not a prelude to the plan. Discovery changes the plan.

The first hiring-team conversation provided new evidence about the actual work: a multi-function AI demand backlog, a vendor-heavy application estate, strict data-loss controls, an approved-tool boundary, and an emerging cross-director team. I updated the operating model rather than protecting the original hypothesis.

Revision recorded · August 3, 2026

My wheelhouse is turning ambiguous work into a visible decision system: listen closely, separate evidence from assumption, map the operating constraints, choose the right intervention, and build only what can be proved and owned.

Original hypothesis

Enter through one workflow

Use a bounded pilot to learn the environment and earn reuse.

Interview signal

Portfolio first

The role must help triage substantial demand across corporate functions.

Accepted adaptation

Decision system before build

Classify capability fit, data, economics, risk, ownership, and capacity.

Still to validate

Internal reality

Specific processes, approval paths, data constraints, and operating cadence remain discovery questions.

Evidence-to-adaptation ledger

Interview signal 01Demand spans multiple corporate functions.Enterprise Change, shared-services finance, procurement, and realty each carry different workflows, systems, owners, and risks.
Accepted adaptationCreate a corporate AI demand portfolio.Compare requests consistently before committing scarce engineering and governance capacity.
Interview signal 02The environment is integration-led, not greenfield.Oracle, Ariba, IBM products, vendor systems, custom services, and shared data shape what is possible.
Accepted adaptationMap systems of record and data lineage first.Model orchestration is secondary to source authority, identity, integration, data transformation, and access.
Interview signal 03The approved capability set matters.M365 Copilot, GitHub Copilot, and vendor features are the practical starting point.
Accepted adaptationAdd a capability-disposition framework.Use existing capability, vendor AI, deterministic automation, bounded custom build, or no AI.
Interview signal 04The team itself is being formed.Several similar roles will operate across peer directors.
Accepted adaptationDesign the SWAT-team operating model.Make intake, ownership, allocation, review cadence, escalation, and reuse explicit.
Interview signal 05Approval speed is an operating constraint.Risk and data-loss controls may prevent a fast production release even when the use case is valuable.
Accepted adaptationProve value in the safest approved mode.Offline evaluation, sandbox, shadow, draft-only, assist, and production pilot are all legitimate evidence postures.

The revised operating sequence

Listen
Map demand
Classify options
Select proof
Evaluate safely
Standardize learning

What this demonstrates

  • Tactical empathy before prescription
  • Evidence boundaries and honest hypotheses
  • Portfolio and systems thinking
  • Business-process and engineering translation
  • Governance attached to real work
  • Learning converted into a durable operating mechanism

What did not change

  • Scale the work—not just the prompt
  • Human authority for consequential decisions
  • Evaluation before expansion
  • Measure completed outcomes
  • Advance, repair, hold, or stop from evidence
Public-evidence boundary: This revision uses broad mandate signals from the hiring conversation. It does not publish proprietary processes, internal performance data, or unverified claims about PNC’s systems or maturity.
