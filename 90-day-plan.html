Operating premise

Understand the portfolio. Enter through one bounded workflow. Earn the reusable pattern.

Discovery changed the plan. The initial hypothesis centered on entering through one bounded workflow. A hiring-team conversation clarified a broader need: first establish a defensible way to triage AI demand across corporate functions, approved tools, vendor platforms, shared data, custom services, and strict control boundaries—then select the safest proof workflow.

DAYS 1–15

Orient to the portfolio and decision system

  • Listen across Enterprise Change, shared-services finance, supply chain/procurement, realty, engineering, architecture, data, security, risk, and adoption partners.
  • Map SWAT-team intake, ownership, decision rights, escalation, capacity, and shared-versus-local responsibilities.
  • Identify how ideas currently move from process pain to an approved delivery path.
DAYS 16–30

Build the demand portfolio and select a bounded proof

  • Inventory requested and existing AI-assisted workflows, systems, owners, overlaps, failure modes, and current maturity.
  • Map systems of record, data lineage, identity, access, vendor capability, integration, observability, and DLP boundaries.
  • Rank candidates by value, capability fit, feasibility, consequence, reversibility, economics, ownership, and change burden.

Discovery outputs by day 30

Corporate AI demand portfolio

Requests classified by business function, owner, workflow, approved capability fit, system dependency, data readiness, consequence, economics, capacity, and recommended disposition.

Decision and SWAT-team map

Who owns business intent, engineering, risk acceptance, source authority, approval, escalation, work allocation, and reuse.

System and lineage map

Systems of record, integrations, field authority, data transformations, access boundaries, vendor features, and unresolved conflicts.

Evaluation contract

Representative cases, expected behavior, failure categories, thresholds, reviewers, recovery, and regression ownership.

Measurement and economics baseline

Cycle time, queue delay, labor, rework, exceptions, adoption, risk, total operating cost, and cost per accepted outcome.

Day-30 decision

Select one or two bounded proof workflows only when accountable owners, approved capability paths, controlled data, representative users, evaluation evidence, human authority, and a reversible operating mode are explicit.

DAYS 31–45

Design the bounded proof

  • Choose the simplest approved solution path: M365 Copilot, GitHub Copilot, vendor feature, deterministic automation, or bounded custom service.
  • Apply the AI Work Control Envelope: business intent, approved context, tool authority, evaluation evidence, human decision rights, and operating record.
  • Define modular workflow, source-lineage, permission, logging, review, fallback, stop, and recovery requirements.
DAYS 46–60

Evaluate in the safest approved mode

  • Exercise normal, missing, conflicting, stale, permission, leakage, injection, tool-failure, and recovery cases.
  • Operate only at the approved posture: offline evaluation, sandbox, shadow, draft-only, assist mode, or production pilot.
  • Observe completed outcomes, corrections, overrides, exceptions, review burden, user trust, and hidden process defects.

Success is production-relevant evidence—not forcing production before the organization is ready.

DAYS 61–75

Repair the operating mechanism

  • Distinguish model or prompt defects from source-data, lineage, process, permission, interface, training, vendor, or ownership defects.
  • Tighten controls, clarify authority and stop conditions, repair the workflow, and rerun the evaluation set.
  • Confirm support, monitoring, incident, change, data-quality, and escalation responsibilities before broader use.
DAYS 76–90

Standardize learning and recommend what advances

  • Separate reusable intake, capability-selection, prompt, retrieval, tool, evaluation, telemetry, and governance patterns from local context.
  • Publish the SWAT-team review mechanism, ownership record, operating cadence, pattern backlog, and next-quarter roadmap.
  • Recommend advance, repair, hold, or stop using like-for-like evidence and PNC’s actual approval criteria.
